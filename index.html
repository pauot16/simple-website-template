Game description

Super Mariano Bros is an horizontal 2D Platform game where you control the displacement of the character but with the inconvenient that once the camera scrolls right you won't be able to go back in the level. In Mariano's adventure you will have to complete the level facing enemies, obstacles, and hidden secrets. The gameplay is iconic, featuring power-ups like the Super Mushroom, Fire Flower, and Starman, which help Mariano defeat enemies and progress through the levels.
